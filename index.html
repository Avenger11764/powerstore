COMMAND CONSOLE

Player Commands

/start

Register player profile and view introduction.

/profile

View coins, cards, active status & God card uses counter.

/store

Open interactive Power Card Store (Private DM only).

/use <CardName> [@target]

Activate a card from your inventory on yourself or target player.

Admin Management Commands

/startevent <event_name>

Triggers any of the 7 global events.

/disablecard <CardName>

Disables a card from store purchase and usage live.

/enablecard <CardName>

Re-enables a previously disabled card.

/award <amount> @username

Award or deduct (negative number) coins from a player.
